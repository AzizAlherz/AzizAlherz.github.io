excerpt: 'This work uses grand-canonical density functional theory to compute the hydricity, bond dissociation free energy, and acidity of chemisorbed hydrogen across transition metal facets. Surface hydricity depends linearly on applied cathodic bias on every facet studied, while the potential dependence of the bond dissociation free energy is set by the metal: nearly constant on Pt, stabilized with potential on Ag and Cu.'
